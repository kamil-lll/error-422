### Summary (kısa açıklama)

```
A Minecraft version that should not exist. It loads, it plays, and it watches back. Horror glitches for Forge 1.21.1. TR/EN.
```

### Açıklama gövdesi

```
> Warning: flashing lights, rapid color changes, screen shake, loud glitch audio and fake crash screens.
> Not suitable for photosensitive players. Every effect can be toned down or disabled in config/error422-common.toml.

Something is wrong with this version.

It boots. The world loads. Everything looks normal — for a while.

Then it stops being normal.

What you need to know

- Minecraft 1.21.1 · Forge 52.x
- Turkish and English — follows your game language
- Works in singleplayer and on servers
- Nothing outside your .minecraft folder is touched; no files are deleted or corrupted

Play it without reading anything else. That's the point of it.
```

## 3) Yükleme sırası

1. https://authors.curseforge.com → **Create Project** (veya CurseForge'da profil → **Author Dashboard**).
2. Yukarıdaki proje alanlarını doldur, avatar + galeriyi yükle.
3. **Files → Upload File** ile jar'ı yükle (sürüm 1.21.1 / Forge / Release).
4. Kaydet → proje **moderasyon kuyruğuna** girer ("pending approval").
5. Onaylanınca proje ve dosya herkese açılır.
